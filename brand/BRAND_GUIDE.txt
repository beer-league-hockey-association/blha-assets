BLHA BRAND GUIDE: 8-BIT ARCADE
==============================
Beer League Hockey Association, est. 2026. Every league graphic is pixel art:
drawn on a small grid of square pixels and enlarged by whole numbers with hard
edges. The files in brand/kit are generated by tools/build_brand_package.py and
tools/build_discord_assets.py (drawing code in tools/blha_pixel.py).

PALETTE
-------
  Black     #0E0F12   grounds, outer keyline, counters
  Charcoal  #2B2D31   main ground (banners, headers, embed side color)
  Gold      #FFB81C   keylines, boards, rules, eyebrows, the accent
  Cream     #F4EFE4   titles on dark, textured paper ground
  Ice       #EEF5FA   rink ice
  Paper     #FCFCFC   letter fill of BLHA and the B
  Blue      #2457C5   rink blue lines, links on the league site
  Red       #C8241F   rink red line; red status stamps
  Status green #3FA34D  stamps only (approved, vote passed, paid in full)

  Tones (one step lighter or darker, for grain, dither and bevels only):
  gold #FFD76A / #C68A00, charcoal #313338 / #26282C, stands #1D1F23,
  steel #9AA1A9 / #6E747C, muted text #B8B9BE.

TYPE RAMP
---------
Fonts (OFL, in brand/fonts with OFL.txt):
  Jersey 10   display: BLHA, titles, stamp words. Pixel = 75 units; caps are 10 pixels tall.
  Silkscreen  labels: eyebrows, sign-off lines, BEER LEAGUE HOCKEY ASSOCIATION, EST. 2026.
              Caps are 5 pixels tall; Bold for the lockup line.
  Pixelify Sans  text: the league site, the Constitution (web, PDF and Google Doc) and
              other long documents. A pixel-style face drawn for reading, with real
              weights from 400 to 700. Set it with generous line spacing.

Render pixel fonts at one export pixel per font pixel (or a whole multiple),
with antialiasing off. Sizes below are export pixels per font pixel:
  Wordmark BLHA        6x the lockup unit (36 on 1920 banners, 42 on the 2000 px wordmark)
  Lockup line + EST    1x the lockup unit (6 on 1920 banners)
  Header title         8 (caps 80 px); 6 or 4 for long titles
  Header eyebrow       4 (Silkscreen, gold)
  Header sign-off      4 (Silkscreen, muted)
  Stamp word           4 to 10, as large as fits; stamp sub-line 3 or 4

GRID
----
  8 px grid: banners, channel headers (200 x 67 pixels for 1600 x 533), dividers and
  stamps. Each header pixel is 2 px wide when Discord shows it at 400 px.
  Avatars and icons: the small 14 x 14 B on a 20 x 20 (avatars) or 16 x 16 (emoji,
  favicon) grid, so they stay sharp at Discord's 16 to 48 px.
  Official seal: 256 x 256 grid at 4 px (1024) or 1 px (256).
  Thin rule: 4 px grid, so its three lines keep their weights.

THE ICE RESURFACER
------------------
  The league's mascot machine: a 56 x 28 pixel side view that drives to the right, with
  BLHA in 3 x 5 pixel letters on the body and the towel trailing behind. Two frames
  (wheels straight, then the body bobbed up 1 pixel) for animation. Its own colors:
  rink blue #2F6FDB, rib blue #7FB7F0, hatch navy #1F4FA8, seat charcoal #3A3D42, plus
  black, paper, gold, steel and cream. Used on the title screen (invite splash), the
  league site's rink, the Constitution cover and the hype video.

SOUND
-----
  The soundboard (brand/sounds) is 8-bit chiptune to match: square and pulse waves, a
  triangle bass and a noise channel, loudness-matched at -14 LUFS, each under 5.2 s.

LOGOS
-----
  Wordmark lockup: BLHA (paper letters, gold then black keyline), then
  BEER LEAGUE HOCKEY ASSOCIATION, then EST. 2026 between two gold rules.
  On light grounds: black letters with a paper (or cream) keyline then gold.
  B mark: the pixel B traced from the official mark (paper, gold, black).
  Large B is 28 x 32 pixels; the site's small B is 14 x 14 for 64 px and below.

  Clear space: keep empty space of at least one quarter of the BLHA letter height
  on every side of the lockup, and one quarter of the B's width around the B.
  Minimum size: lockup 192 px wide (lockup unit 1); B mark 16 px (small B).

DO
--
  Enlarge only by whole numbers with nearest-neighbor scaling.
  Keep the keyline order: gold then black on paper letters.
  Use the full lockup (BLHA over the name over EST. 2026) where a full lockup is needed.
  Use status colors by meaning: green = approved/passed/paid, red = denied/failed/owed/urgent,
  gold = pending/amended/champions, cream = official/final/rulings.
  Bump the ?v= version (tools/discohook_format.py ASSET_VERSION) whenever hosted art changes.

DON'T
-----
  Don't smooth, blur, anti-alias, rotate or stretch pixel art (the seal ring text is the
  only rotated text, re-thresholded to solid pixels).
  Don't put team colors on league marks, banners, headers, dividers or stamps. Team
  colors appear only on small jersey or stripe elements (a team chip, a jersey stripe).
  Don't stack the B above BLHA; the B and the wordmark are separate marks.
  Don't recolour the B or BLHA, or swap in non-pixel fonts.
  Don't mix the small 14 x 14 B and the large B in one graphic.
  Don't use status green anywhere except stamps.
