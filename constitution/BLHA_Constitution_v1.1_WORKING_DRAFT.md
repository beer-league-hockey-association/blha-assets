# Beer League Hockey Association Constitution

**Working Draft — Version 1.1**  
**Established:** 2026  
**Status:** Working draft for Discord publication; subject to revision before final ratification.

## Quick Reference
- 12-franchise dynasty fantasy hockey league on Fantrax.
- Format: H2H Points, daily lineups, 22-week regular season, each opponent twice, no divisions.
- Active roster: 20 — 3 C, 3 LW, 3 RW, 3 F, 6 D, 2 G.
- Reserve: 6. Minors: 10. IR: 5.
- Minor eligibility: age 25 or younger as of Sept. 15; skaters 100 or fewer career NHL regular-season GP; goalies 50 or fewer.
- FAAB: $1,000 per season, no rollover, $0 bids allowed, hidden bids, daily processing, no FCFS.
- External acquisitions: 5 per normal fantasy week.
- Goalie starts: maximum 4 in a normal week; 8 in the two-week championship.
- Trade deadline: end of Week 20; trading reopens the day after the Stanley Cup Final.
- Annual draft: 5 rounds, linear, approximately 2–3 weeks after the NHL Entry Draft.
- Playoffs: 6 teams, top 2 receive byes, reseeding enabled, 1-week quarterfinals and semifinals, 2-week cumulative championship.
- Annual dues: $50 per franchise. Startup payment: $100, covering the first season plus the following season in advance.

## Article I — League Identity and Purpose
The Beer League Hockey Association (BLHA) is a 12-franchise dynasty fantasy hockey league designed around long-term roster construction, prospect development, active trading, daily competition, and permanent league history.

Fantrax is the authoritative platform for gameplay, rosters, scoring, transactions, standings, and playoff results. Discord is the league’s communication, governance, trade-market, scouting, recordkeeping, and community platform.

## Article II — Governance and League Authority
The Commissioner administers the league, maintains Fantrax settings, publishes official rulings, enforces the Constitution, and protects the integrity of the league. Assistant Commissioners may perform duties delegated by the Commissioner.

Each franchise receives one formal league vote regardless of whether it has one owner or multiple co-owners. Co-owners may fully participate in team management and league discussion, but do not create an additional franchise vote.

Material rule changes should be handled through formal league governance and recorded in the Constitution and voting record. The current published Constitution controls when a conflict exists between old messages, informal discussions, or outdated documents.

## Article III — Franchise Ownership, Dues, and Prizes
Annual dues are $50 per franchise. At league startup, each franchise pays $100, covering the first season and the following season in advance. The league is intended to remain one season prepaid thereafter.

A franchise is a single dues obligation even when co-owners are present.

Current annual allocation from 12 paid franchises is $600. Fantrax Premium is budgeted at $129.95. The current prize structure totals $470: League Champion $250; Runner-Up $100; Presidents’ Trophy $75; Third Place $25; Regular-Season Fantasy Points Leader $20. Any residual amount remains league treasury unless otherwise approved.

## Article IV — League Format and Schedule
The BLHA uses Head-to-Head Points scoring with daily lineups. The regular season is 22 fantasy weeks. Each franchise plays every other franchise twice. The league does not use divisions or conferences.

Individual players lock at approximately one minute before their NHL game begins, subject to Fantrax system behavior.

## Article V — Rosters and Positions
Normal roster limits are 20 active players and 6 reserves, for 26 non-minor controlled roster spots, plus 10 minor-league spots and up to 5 IR spots.

Active positions are: 3 C, 3 LW, 3 RW, 3 F, 6 D, and 2 G.

Minor-league players do not count toward the normal 26-player roster limit. IR players do not count toward normal roster limitations while properly placed on IR, subject to Fantrax eligibility rules.

## Article VI — Minor-League Eligibility
The BLHA maintains 10 minor-league roster spots.

A player must be age 25 or younger using age as of Sept. 15 immediately before the season. That age determination remains fixed for the entire fantasy season.

Skaters remain minor-eligible through 100 career NHL regular-season games played. Goalies remain minor-eligible through 50 career NHL regular-season games played. Career NHL GP continues to accumulate during the season.

Once Fantrax identifies a minor-league player as ineligible, the franchise has three calendar days to promote, trade, drop, or otherwise remove the player from the minor slot. Promotion does not permanently burn minor eligibility; a still-eligible player may later return to minors where Fantrax permits.

Categories not listed above are disabled unless the Constitution is formally amended.

## Article VIII — Lineups and Goalie Starts
Lineups are set daily. Managers are responsible for maintaining legal rosters and active lineups.

A franchise may receive credit for a maximum of 4 goalie starts during a normal fantasy week and 8 goalie starts during the two-week championship period. Fantrax enforcement settings govern the technical application of these limits.

## Article IX — Waivers, FAAB, and Acquisitions
Each franchise receives $1,000 FAAB at the beginning of each season. FAAB does not roll over. $0 bids are permitted and bids use $1 increments. Bids are hidden. Vickrey/proxy bidding is not used.

Unowned players are acquired through FAAB rather than first-come, first-served acquisition. Waivers are intended to process daily at approximately 11:00 AM Eastern Time. Waiver churning protections and the Fantrax waiver-period protections remain enabled where available.

A franchise may make up to 5 external player acquisitions in a normal fantasy week. Championship-period acquisition handling will follow the announced Fantrax configuration for that season.

The consolation-bracket champion receives a $50 FAAB bonus for the following season, giving that franchise $1,050 for that season only. The bonus expires with ordinary current-season FAAB and may be traded as current-season FAAB.

## Article X — Trades and Trade Deadline
Trades are unlimited and are not subject to league vote or veto. A trade is not reversible merely because the Commissioner or other managers consider it lopsided.

Commissioner intervention is limited to narrow circumstances such as collusion, prohibited outside consideration, clear misconduct, or a promptly reported and credible misclick or platform error.

Tradeable assets are rostered players, eligible draft picks, and current-season FAAB. Outside assets, cash consideration, loans, rentals, predetermined tradebacks, and other off-platform consideration are prohibited.

The trade deadline is the end of Week 20. Trading reopens the day after the Stanley Cup Final.

## Article XI — Future Draft Picks and Prepayment
Fantrax draft-pick trading is enabled for the current annual draft plus the next three annual drafts. Five rounds of annual draft picks are tradeable.

Conditional draft picks are prohibited.

A franchise trading a future first- or second-round pick must be prepaid through the season associated with that pick. Trading future third-, fourth-, or fifth-round picks does not trigger additional prepayment beyond the league’s ordinary prepaid requirement.

Current-season FAAB may be traded. Future-season FAAB may not be traded.

## Article XII — Startup Draft
The working startup format is a 36-round slow snake draft with a 4-hour pick clock. A nightly sleep window runs from midnight through 8:00 AM Eastern Time. Picks may be made during the sleep window where Fantrax allows, but the active clock is paused.

Draft queues are strongly encouraged. After two timer expirations, Fantrax auto-draft behavior may be used until the manager returns. Final startup mechanics remain subject to the tested Fantrax configuration before the inaugural draft begins.

## Article XIII — Annual Draft
The annual BLHA draft is 5 rounds and linear rather than snake. It is expected to begin approximately 2–3 weeks after the NHL Entry Draft.

The draft uses an 8-hour pick clock with a midnight–8:00 AM Eastern Time pause. Managers are encouraged to maintain queues. Timeout handling may use Fantrax queue/autodraft or temporary-skip tools as announced before the draft.

The player pool is not restricted to the current NHL draft class. Any unowned player in the Fantrax player pool who satisfies BLHA minor-league eligibility may be selected.

## Article XIV — Regular Season and Standings
The regular season consists of 22 scoring periods. Every franchise plays every other franchise twice.

Regular-season standings and head-to-head results are recorded by Fantrax. The Presidents’ Trophy is awarded based on the league’s configured regular-season H2H record criteria. The Regular-Season Fantasy Points Leader prize is based on fantasy points accumulated during the regular season only.

## Article XV — Playoffs and Consolation
Six franchises qualify for the championship playoffs. Seeds 1 and 2 receive first-round byes. The opening round is Seed 3 vs. Seed 6 and Seed 4 vs. Seed 5. The bracket reseeds after each round.

Quarterfinals and semifinals are one fantasy week each. The championship is a two-week cumulative matchup. If a playoff matchup ends in an exact tie, the higher seed advances or wins.

The championship schedule should end approximately six days before the NHL regular season concludes to reduce late-season NHL lineup volatility.

The six non-championship teams participate in the consolation bracket. The consolation champion receives the next-season $50 FAAB bonus described in Article IX.

## Article XVI — Competitive Integrity and Anti-Tanking
Managers are expected to compete in good faith and maintain legal, reasonably competitive lineups. Deliberate lineup sabotage, collusion, coordinated dumping of assets, or manipulation intended to distort standings, playoff qualification, or draft position is prohibited.

Where the league uses Max PF or a similar anti-tanking measure for draft-order purposes, Fantrax and the league’s published annual draft-order procedure control. Temporary lineup decisions, rebuilding strategies, and lopsided-looking trades do not by themselves establish misconduct.

## Article XVII — Orphans, Commissioner Powers, and Amendments
If a franchise becomes orphaned, the Commissioner may recruit and approve a replacement owner and take reasonable temporary steps necessary to preserve the roster and league schedule. Any prepaid league obligation attached to the franchise remains part of the franchise unless the league formally determines otherwise.

The Commissioner may correct clerical errors, Fantrax configuration mistakes, or platform issues necessary to apply an already-existing rule. Such corrections are not authority to create new substantive rules midstream.

Formal amendments should be recorded in the Constitution channel with the affected section, approval date, and effective date. The latest published version of the Constitution is the controlling league document.
